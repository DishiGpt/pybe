# Verified Himalayan Route & Elevation Profile (`docs/altitudes.md`)

All elevations and route sequences along the 474 km Manali–Leh Highway (NH-3) have been verified against official **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)** project signboards (Projects *Deepak* and *Himank*), **Survey of India (SOI)** topographic maps, and Himachal Pradesh/Ladakh Tourism Department records.
